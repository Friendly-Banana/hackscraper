About Event

​Where your ideas meet people who can build them.

​We’re hosting a small builder sprint in a bright industrial loft in Munich, the kind of place where early startups accidentally begin.

​In 3 hours we build something small but real: a tool, a prototype, a micro-product, whatever you’re excited about.
Solo or with people you meet on the spot.

​Light chaos. Good energy. Zero pressure.

​TIMETABLE:

​15:00 — Come in & get settled
15:30 — We start building
18:30 — Demos
19:00 — Connect with other builders

​For anyone who enjoys creating, exploring, or learning. Technical and non-technical people welcome. (20 spots)

​LOCATION:

​Lochhamer Schlag 11a, 82166, Gräfelfing

​Bring your computer and whatever you like to build with.
We’ve got snacks and drinks sorted.

​Hope you can make it!

​Ben Ulrich
